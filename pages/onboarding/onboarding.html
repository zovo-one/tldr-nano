The part cloud-AI tools get wrong

Summarize a page without shipping it to a server.

Every cloud summarizer charges per token, makes you log in, and sends your reading history to a backend you can't audit. TLDR Nano ships none of that. The model runs in your browser's on-device runtime - no API key, no account, nothing leaves the tab.

Click the toolbar icon, click Summarize, and a three-sentence TL;DR appears in two to four seconds. The hard trade-off is that Nano (the on-device model) is weaker at long-form than cloud LLMs. We mitigate that with chunked summary-of-summaries so the arc of the whole document holds.

One click, two seconds

See the TL;DR appear in the side panel.

Click the toolbar icon, then click Summarize page. The side panel opens, content extraction runs against the active tab via activeTab, and the on-device model returns a three-sentence TL;DR. Select text first and click Summarize selection to scope the summary to just what you highlighted.

The status indicator in the side panel moves through idle, extracting, summarizing, and done - so you always know what's happening. If the page is too long, we chunk it into ~3000-char blocks and produce a summary-of-summaries so the whole-document arc holds.
