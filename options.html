How summaries get made

TLDR Nano summarizes the page or text selection in your active tab using your browser's built-in Summarizer API (Chrome 127+). The model runs entirely on your device. Page text is processed in the side panel and discarded - no server, no account, no API key, no network egress.

Keep your reading on your device

Does not handle user data. Defaults and recent-summaries history live in browser storage on your machine. The only network call is license verification, gated on Pro license entry - it never fires for free users.
